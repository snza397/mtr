## The Features That Actually Matter

### Analytics That Don't Disappear

On the free plan, you get 30 days of history. On any paid plan, it's **unlimited** — which means you can go back six months, a year, compare how last quarter stacked up against this one, and actually spot patterns. This is probably the single biggest reason people upgrade from free.

### Competitor Tracking

Even the free plan gives you 5 competitor slots. Paid plans expand this to 100 profiles. You can see how often competitors post, what formats they use, and how their engagement trends over time. Useful for benchmarking and figuring out where you're over- or underperforming.

### SmartLinks (Link in Bio, Done Properly)

Metricool has its own link-in-bio tool called SmartLinks. Unlike standalone tools, your SmartLinks click data lives inside Metricool alongside all your other social analytics — so you can actually connect the dots between link clicks and content performance.

### Post Approval Workflows

Available on Advanced and Custom. If you're managing a client account and someone needs to sign off before anything goes live, this is a genuinely smooth workflow. You can also assign specific roles — editor, reviewer, publisher — so the right people control the right things.

### Ads Management

One underrated feature: Metricool pulls in your Google Ads, Facebook Ads, and TikTok Ads data into the same dashboard. So instead of bouncing between platforms to see whether your boosted posts are actually doing anything, it's right there next to your organic numbers.
